About the show

Signal, not noise.

The AI media cycle is loud and mostly wrong. Every week there's a new claim about what's going to be automated, eliminated, or revolutionized — and most of it evaporates on contact with reality.

AI Coffee Hour is the conversation that happens after the conference ends — when practitioners talk about what's actually running in production, what keeps failing, and what the economic history of automation tells us that the breathless headlines don't. Technology, economics, philosophy — no agenda except honesty.

01

Wide-ranging guests

Industry veterans, researchers, and practitioners from AI, data infrastructure, and enterprise operations — each bringing a different vantage point on the same set of questions.

02

No pitch. No panel.

Conversations run long and go off-script. The interesting part is usually the tangent — and there's no moderator pulling anyone back to the bullet points.

03

AI, economics, and work

The through-line: what does automation actually do to how people work, and what does 250 years of precedent say about where it goes? The history is more useful than the forecast.
